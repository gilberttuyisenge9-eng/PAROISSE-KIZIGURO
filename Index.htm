Amakuru

Parruwasi ya KIZIGURO iherereye mu Intara y’Uburasirazuba, Akarere ka Gatsibo, Umurenge wa Kiziguro, Akagali k’Agakomeye. Ni umuryango w’abakirisitu ukorera ibikorwa by’inyigisho, gusenga no gufasha abaturage.

Serivisi

  • Gusenga no kubwiriza ijambo ry’Imana
  • Uburezi n’ubuto bw’abana n’urubyiruko
  • Imfashanyo ku baturage bakeneye
  • Ibikorwa by’umuryango n’imiryango
